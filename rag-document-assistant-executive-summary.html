A grounded document assistant for faster, more reliable answers

Turning maintenance and quality guidance into an accessible business resource

THE PROBLEM

Teams lose time locating the right policy or procedure across long reference documents. Inconsistent answers create avoidable delays and rework.

THE SOLUTION

A chat experience that searches approved vehicle-safety and manufacturing-quality documents, then returns an answer with the supporting source.

BUSINESS IMPACT

Employees get a consistent starting point for questions. Leaders gain confidence that guidance reflects approved internal material and can scale across teams.

Scope: a decision-support assistant that directs people to the relevant source. It does not replace expert review for consequential decisions.
